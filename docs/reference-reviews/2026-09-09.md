## REF-001 — provider recovery policy

Reference: winbox `d090afc8bef2915f3b29cd92a7f1a8d0c9e06955`,
`src/TvBoxWin.App/SourceHealthTracker.cs` and
`tests/TvBoxWin.App.Tests/SourceHealthTrackerTests.cs`.

Useful idea: health records should age out, and temporary exclusion should have a recovery cycle.
TV already owns this abstraction in `libraries/source-core/ProviderHealthPolicy`; Android
`api/provider/ProviderClient` consumes it for provider calls and proxy calls. No new module,
dependency, or platform adapter was necessary.

Before: two failed calls could remain in memory indefinitely, making an unrelated later failure
the third strike. In-flight failures during a cooldown could accumulate toward another block or
extend the existing one.

After: a 45-minute gap since the last failure resets the streak; cooldown expiry starts fresh;
late failures during an active cooldown are ignored. Success and explicit clear retain their
existing reset behavior. Clock rollback resets stale state. Inactive records are pruned on failure.
TV keeps its existing three-failure threshold and 60-second cooldown.

This is an independently written adaptation of the lifecycle idea, not copied C# source.
Winbox's persistent blacklist, error-category thresholds and multi-day exponential cooldowns
were not adopted: those introduce separate product decisions and do not fit TV's existing
short-lived provider circuit directly.
